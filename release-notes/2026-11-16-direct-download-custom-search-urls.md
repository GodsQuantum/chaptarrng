Direct Download now supports operator-supplied catalog and mirror search URL templates and an optional FlareSolverr service for browser-verification challenges. It reports rejected SeerrNG service-key requests with the method and path and marks failed sources unhealthy. Configure FlareSolverr on a trusted network; some challenges may still fail.
