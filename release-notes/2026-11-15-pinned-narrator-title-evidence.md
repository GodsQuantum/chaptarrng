Automatic searches for an audiobook pinned to one narrator now reject a release whose title names the narrator of another edition of the same book, such as a scene release ending in "Rob.Inglis" for a book pinned to Andy Serkis. Before, only explicit narrator metadata counted, so these releases could be grabbed and imported. A manual search can still grab them.
